# PharmaLens privacy policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

PharmaLens is a medicine safety assistant for Lebanon, made by **TechWrap LB** (Beirut, Lebanon). It is an individual initiative: it is not the Ministry of Public Health (MOPH) app and is not affiliated with the Ministry. This policy explains what the app keeps on your phone, what it sends to other services, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- PharmaLens has **no account, no server of its own, no ads and no analytics**. We do not sell or share your data.
- Your health profiles, prescriptions, reminders and dose history are **saved only on your phone**.
- To read a prescription or answer a question, the app sends the photo or the question to **Google's Gemini AI**. To find pharmacies near you, it sends your location to **Google Places**.
- You can delete your data at any time: **Settings > Delete all my data**, or uninstall the app.

## What stays on your phone

The app saves the following on your device only. We cannot see it.

- **Health profiles** for you and the family members you add: names you type, allergies, health conditions, age (stored as a birth year), sex, height and weight.
- **Scanned prescriptions**: the photo, the medicines read from it, the doctor's name and specialty when written, and who the prescription is for.
- **Medicine reminders** and the history of doses taken, skipped or snoozed.
- **Saved medicine pages** (patient information the app downloaded), the assistant conversation while the app is open, and your settings (language, theme, alarm sound).

## What is sent to other services, and why

The app talks directly to these services. Each one receives only what the feature needs, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

- **Google Gemini API (Google LLC)**
  - When you scan a prescription: the photo and the text the phone read from it, so the AI can transcribe the medicines and directions.
  - When you use the assistant: your messages, any photo you attach, and the information the assistant looks up to answer you. When your question is about your own medicines, that can include the allergies, conditions, reminders and recent prescriptions saved for the selected person.
  - On a medicine page: the medicine's name, ingredients and label text, to write a short summary in English and Arabic.
  - Google processes this under the [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Under those terms, content sent through unpaid (free tier) access may be used by Google to improve its products; paid access is not used that way.
- **Google Places API (Google LLC)**
  - When you open the pharmacies map: your current location, to find pharmacies near you. When you search the map: the words you type.
  - When a doctor's hospital is shown: the hospital's name, to find its phone number and address.
  - The app asks for location only while you use the map, never in the background.
- **openFDA (U.S. Food and Drug Administration)**: the active ingredients of a medicine you open, to find its official U.S. label.
- **OpenStreetMap**: the map images for the area you are looking at. Like any website, the tile server sees your internet address.
- **GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com)**: the app downloads the latest official MOPH price list from our public repository. GitHub sees your internet address; nothing about you is sent.
- **WhatsApp**: only if you choose to contact us or to report an AI answer. A report contains the AI's answer and your reason, not your question or health data.

## Permissions

- **Camera and photos**: only when you take or choose a prescription photo, through Android's own picker.
- **Location** (while using the app): to show nearby pharmacies.
- **Notifications, alarms and reminders, full-screen alarms, start at boot**: to remind you of your doses on time, ring like an alarm when you choose that, and keep your reminders after the phone restarts.

## AI can make mistakes

Prescription readings, medicine summaries and assistant answers are produced by AI and can be wrong. PharmaLens is a helper, not a doctor or a pharmacist, and it is not a medical device. Always check medicines, doses and directions with your doctor or pharmacist. Medicine prices come from the official list published on the MOPH website (moph.gov.lb).

## Children

PharmaLens is meant for adults managing their own or their family's medicines. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. A parent may add a child's profile; that profile stays on the parent's phone like all other data.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete one scan, reminder or family member at any time, or everything at once in **Settings > Delete all my data**. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Because we have no server or account, there is nothing to delete on our side. Data sent to Google or the FDA for a request is handled under their own policies.

## Security

All connections use HTTPS. Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox. Keep your phone locked to protect your health information.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the text in the app.
